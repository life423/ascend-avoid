How to Play:

Hop around the arena, grab gems and dodge the traffic. Everyone on the site plays in the same world, and bots (marked with a robot) fill in when it's quiet.

  • Arrow keys or WASD to move (diagonals too)
  • Dash into someone to shove them
  • Hold the mouse button to steer toward the cursor
  • Press space to dash (a quick burst that costs a gem)
  • When the arena shifts, get onto the new floor and don't get shoved off

A hit sprays out half your gems. With none left, you're out for two seconds.

Solo is the classic game: reach the top without getting hit, and press R to restart.
